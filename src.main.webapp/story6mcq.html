The Thirsty Crow - Story Summary

📖 Saransh (Summary)

Once upon a time, during a scorching summer, a thirsty crow searched everywhere for water and finally found a pitcher with very little water at the bottom that he could not reach.

Showing incredible intelligence and patience, the clever crow picked up small pebbles one by one and dropped them into the pitcher. As the pebbles accumulated at the bottom, the water level gradually rose to the top.

The crow drank the water and quenched his thirst completely, proving that where there is a will, intelligence and persistent effort will always find a way to succeed.
