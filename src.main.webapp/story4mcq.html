📖 Story Summary: The Great Miller Family Wedding Debacle

Overview: This comedic story revolves around the Miller family in suburban Ohio preparing for the wedding of Arthur and Chloe. What was supposed to be a simple backyard ceremony turns into total chaos due to overbearing and eccentric family members.

Key Highlights: Martha (Arthur's mother) tries to run the event using color-coded binders; Leo (the tech-savvy brother) attempts to deliver rings via drones while avoiding a dog allergic to them; Uncle Bob builds a dangerously flimsy wooden arch with duct tape; and Aunt Clara bakes cardboard-like vegan cupcakes. Amidst all the flying cupcakes, barking dogs, and structural collapses, Arthur and Chloe embrace the utter madness with laughter, realizing their love can survive anything.
