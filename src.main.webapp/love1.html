The Harvest Love Story

Story Summary

The Harvest Love Story follows the romance between Ethan and Chloe, who meet while working together during the sugarcane harvesting season. Bonding through mutual help and shared moments in the fields, they promise to stay faithful to each other as the season ends. Ultimately, they reunite as life partners for the next harvest, celebrating a love rooted deeply in purity of heart.
