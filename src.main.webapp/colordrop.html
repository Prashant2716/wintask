🎨 Color Drop

Rules: Color Drop is a simple block-matching puzzle game. A colored block appears from the top of the board and you need to place it in an empty column. Try to create three or more blocks of the same color in a horizontal or vertical line. Matching blocks will disappear and give you points and coins.

Controls: On desktop, use the mouse to drag the falling block left or right. You can also use the Left and Right Arrow keys to move the block. Press Space to drop the block immediately. On mobile, touch and drag the block to the position where you want to place it.

Tips: Plan your moves carefully and keep the board as clear as possible. Try to build matching colors instead of filling random columns. Making more matches helps you increase your score and earn coins while keeping the game going for longer.
