The Mystery of the Space Needle Secret

Story Summary

Living in Seattle, two school friends Lucas and Harper are walking past the Space Needle after school when Lucas's sneaker scrapes against an old mysterious brass box hidden under a pile of leaves. It has an engraved riddle: "Where the needles touch the sky, look below to find what money cannot buy." Using information from their recent school project about the 1962 World's Fair, Harper figures out the combination and unlocks the box.

Inside the box, instead of gold or coins, they find a vintage Polaroid camera, a notebook filled with adventurous notes from students dating back to 1985, and an instruction card. The instructions require them to take a photo of the Space Needle at sunset, write their future dreams in the notebook, and re-hide the box for the next finders. With a beautiful sunset backdrop, Lucas takes the photo, marking the beginning of an exciting adventure.
